ACADEMIC WELLNESS KNOWLEDGE BASE

SLEEP AND ACADEMIC WELLNESS

Adequate and consistent sleep is important for concentration, memory, learning efficiency, and academic performance. Students experiencing insufficient sleep should maintain a regular sleep schedule, avoid unnecessary late-night screen exposure, and create a consistent study and rest routine.


STRESS MANAGEMENT

High academic stress can negatively affect concentration, motivation, productivity, and overall academic wellness. Students experiencing elevated stress should divide large academic tasks into smaller activities, maintain a realistic study schedule, take short breaks, and use relaxation activities such as deep breathing or light physical activity.


SCREEN TIME MANAGEMENT

Excessive screen time, particularly during study breaks and before sleep, can negatively affect concentration and sleep quality. Students with high screen exposure should take regular breaks, reduce unnecessary recreational screen usage, and maintain dedicated periods for offline study and rest.


PHYSICAL ACTIVITY

Regular physical activity can support energy levels, concentration, mood, and general well-being. Students with low physical activity should incorporate suitable movement into their daily routine, such as walking, stretching, or other age-appropriate physical activities.


STUDY HOURS AND TIME MANAGEMENT

Long or poorly structured study sessions can contribute to fatigue and reduced productivity. Students with heavy study schedules should divide their study time into manageable sessions, include appropriate breaks, prioritize important tasks, and maintain a balanced daily routine.


ASSIGNMENT WORKLOAD

A high assignment workload can increase academic pressure when multiple tasks are handled simultaneously. Students experiencing a heavy workload should prioritize assignments according to deadlines and importance, divide larger tasks into smaller steps, and maintain a planned study schedule.


ACADEMIC PERFORMANCE

Academic performance can be influenced by study habits, workload, sleep, stress, and overall academic wellness. Students experiencing difficulty in academic performance should identify areas requiring improvement, maintain consistent study practices, seek academic guidance when necessary, and avoid relying only on last-minute preparation.


ATTENDANCE AND ACADEMIC PARTICIPATION

Regular attendance and participation can help students remain connected with academic content and classroom activities. Students with low attendance should try to maintain consistent participation and review missed academic material through appropriate institutional resources.


BALANCED ACADEMIC ROUTINE

A balanced routine should provide sufficient time for studying, sleeping, physical activity, relaxation, and personal activities. Students should avoid excessive academic workload without adequate rest and should maintain a consistent daily schedule.


GENERAL ACADEMIC WELLNESS

Academic wellness involves maintaining a healthy balance between academic responsibilities and personal well-being. Students should monitor their study patterns, sleep, stress, screen exposure, physical activity, workload, and academic performance. When several risk factors are present together, students should consider making gradual improvements to their daily routine and seek appropriate support when needed.
